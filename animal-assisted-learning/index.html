🐾 Animal Assisted Learning

Working with animals builds empathy, emotional regulation and a sense of responsibility. Our AAL sessions offer a calm, therapeutic environment where young people open up, connect and grow at their own pace.

Make a Referral Safeguarding & Compliance

What Animal Assisted Learning Involves

Animal Assisted Learning (AAL) uses structured, supervised contact with animals to help young people build trust, communication and emotional regulation skills that can be difficult to access through conventional classroom-based approaches. Sessions are calm, unhurried and led at the young person's own pace, giving them a genuinely non-judgemental space to open up.

Every AAL session is led by a trained handler, with animal welfare, hygiene and allergy checks built into our planning for every referral before a session takes place. Sessions can run as a standalone provision or alongside tuition, Life Skills or Boxing Fitness as part of a wider personalised plan.

Who This Is For

SEMH Needs

Young people with social, emotional and mental health needs who benefit from a calm, low-pressure route into engagement.

Anxiety & Trauma Histories

A therapeutic, trust-building environment for young people who find traditional settings overwhelming.

School Refusal & EBSA

A gentle re-engagement point for young people experiencing emotionally-based school avoidance.

Communication Difficulties

Non-verbal, relationship-based connection for young people who struggle with conventional social interaction.

Safeguarding & Delivery

Animal handling is always led by a trained handler, with a written risk assessment covering animal behaviour, hygiene and allergy checks in place for every young person referred into AAL.

As with every L-Fit Pathways provision, Animal Assisted Learning is delivered under the direct oversight of our joint Designated Safeguarding Leads, Adam Byrne and Craig Lloyd, both Level 5 Safeguarding/DSL trained and Enhanced DBS checked, in line with KCSIE 2026 and our full Safeguarding & Compliance framework.
