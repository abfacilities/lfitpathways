About L-Fit Pathways

A Different Kind of Learning

“We believe every young person deserves a pathway — no matter where they are starting from.”

Founded by co-directors Adam Byrne and Craig Lloyd, L-Fit Pathways is an alternative provision based in Bootle, Liverpool. We support young people aged 5 to 25 who have not yet found the right environment to thrive — offering structured, compassionate, experience-based learning with safeguarding at the centre of everything we do.

  • ✓ Trauma-informed, relationship-based practice at our core
  • ✓ KCSIE 2026 compliant — full safeguarding and SEND policies in place
  • ✓ One-to-one and small-group delivery tailored to each individual
  • ✓ Structured reintegration pathways back into education or training
  • ✓ Deep community roots across Bootle, Sefton and Liverpool
  • ✓ Supporting young people at risk of becoming NEET
🛡️

Safeguarding & Compliance

Trust Is Built In, Not Bolted On

L-Fit Pathways operates as an unregistered alternative provision within DfE thresholds (under 18 hours per week), with full safeguarding, data protection and health & safety policies held and reviewed regularly.

Designated Safeguarding Leads

Both co-directors are joint DSLs, Level 5 Safeguarding/DSL trained and hold current Enhanced DBS checks.

Our Core Provisions

Five Pathways to Progress

Each provision is designed to meet a young person where they are and move them forward — emotionally, academically, physically and practically — within a fully safeguarded environment.

01
Emotional & Social

🐾 Animal Assisted Learning

Working with animals builds empathy, emotional regulation and a sense of responsibility. Our AAL sessions offer a calm, therapeutic environment where young people open up, connect and grow at their own pace — particularly effective for those with SEMH needs, anxiety or trauma histories.

Learn more →
02
Wellbeing & Resilience

🌳 Forest School

Qualified Forest School delivery in a natural outdoor setting. Young people develop problem-solving, teamwork, independence and resilience through guided outdoor experiences — away from the pressure of a traditional classroom. Certificated through recognised Forest School frameworks.

Learn more →
03
Academic Progress

✏️ Educational Tuition

Personalised one-to-one and small-group tuition across core subjects including English, Maths and Science. We work to school or college targets, individual learning plans and EHCPs, ensuring genuine academic progress alongside therapeutic provision.

Learn more →
04
Physical & Mental Health

🥊 Boxing Fitness & Discipline

Non-contact boxing training that provides structure, discipline and a healthy physical outlet. Proven to improve focus, respect and self-esteem — especially for young people who struggle to engage with conventional activities. Builds the mental resilience to take on daily challenges.

Learn more →
05
Independence & Confidence

🧩 Life Skills

Practical, real-world sessions covering communication, budgeting, cooking, personal safety, travel training and independence skills. Essential preparation for young people transitioning to adulthood — particularly those at risk of becoming NEET or leaving education without functional life skills.

Learn more →
06
Transition Planning

🔄 Reintegration & Transition

Structured support for young people working toward returning to a mainstream or alternative setting, progressing to college, or moving into employment. Includes progress reporting, stakeholder liaison and a personalised transition plan co-produced with the young person and their family.

Who We Support

Young People Who Need a Different Way

We work with young people from 5 to 25 across a broad range of need — whether they are excluded, home-educated, at risk of becoming NEET, or simply struggling to engage in their current setting.

Age Groups & Key Stages

KS1Ages 5–7

Early Years Transition

Nurture-based early intervention for young children struggling to settle.

KS2Ages 7–11

Primary Alternative Provision

Structured support alongside or instead of primary schooling.

KS3Ages 11–14

Secondary Transition

Engagement programmes and emotional support through secondary entry.

KS4Ages 14–16

GCSE & Qualification Pathways

Tuition aligned to GCSE targets alongside therapeutic provision.

KS5Ages 16–19

Post-16 & College Support

Alternative to or alongside college, vocational readiness and progression.

NEETAges 16–25

NEET Prevention & Re-engagement

Life skills, employment readiness and structured re-engagement pathways.

We Support Young People With

School Refusal & EBSA

Emotionally-based school avoidance addressed through gradual, therapeutic re-engagement at the young person’s pace.

Exclusion (Fixed Term or Permanent)

Responsive alternative provision during exclusion periods with structured reintegration planning from day one.

SEND & Complex Needs

Experience across ADHD, autism, trauma, attachment difficulties, learning differences and SEMH needs.

Elective Home Education (EHE)

Supplementary or full provision for home-educated young people, including PE, socialisation and tuition.

EOTAS & EHCP Placements

Education Other Than At School and EHCP-funded placements with progress reporting and review attendance.

NEET & Post-16 Disengagement

Young people aged 16–25 who are not in education, employment or training and need a structured pathway forward.

How It Works

From Referral to Progress

A clear, four-step pathway from first contact to active provision.

01

Submit a Referral

Complete our online referral form below. Parents, schools, colleges or professionals can all refer. We respond within 2 working days.

02

Initial Meeting

A no-pressure initial meeting to understand the young person’s needs, strengths, goals and current situation.

03

Personalised Plan

A tailored provision plan co-produced with the young person, their family and any involved professionals or LA contacts.

04

Active Provision

Provision begins with regular progress reviews, written reports and a clear forward pathway.
