👥 Meet the Team

L-Fit Pathways is led day-to-day by our two co-directors, who are also our joint Designated Safeguarding Leads. Every session, referral and record runs under their direct oversight.

AB

Adam Byrne

Co-Director & Designated Safeguarding Lead

Co-founded L-Fit Pathways to give young people who haven't yet found the right environment a structured, compassionate way back into learning. Level 5 Safeguarding/DSL trained and Enhanced DBS checked.
