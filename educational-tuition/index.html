✏️ Educational Tuition

Personalised one-to-one and small-group tuition across core subjects including English, Maths and Science. We work to school or college targets, individual learning plans and EHCPs, ensuring genuine academic progress alongside therapeutic provision.

What Educational Tuition Involves

Our tuition sessions are built around each young person's current level, not their year group label. Sessions are planned to school or college targets, Individual Learning Plans and EHCP outcomes where these exist, so progress made with us maps directly back to the young person's wider education plan.

Delivery is one-to-one or in small groups, with sessions structured to rebuild a young person's relationship with learning as much as their subject knowledge — particularly important for young people who have experienced repeated setbacks, exclusion, or long periods out of education.

Who This Is For

Falling Behind Academically

Young people who have missed significant learning time and need focused, individual catch-up support.

EHCP & EOTAS Placements

Tuition planned directly around EHCP outcomes and Education Other Than At School arrangements.

Excluded or Home Educated

Continued academic progress during exclusion periods or as part of elective home education.

Exam & Qualification Pathways

Structured, targeted support working toward GCSE or equivalent qualification outcomes.

Safeguarding & Delivery

One-to-one tuition is delivered in visible, open settings wherever possible, with safeguarding of individual sessions built into our staff induction and session planning.

As with every L-Fit Pathways provision, Educational Tuition is delivered under the direct oversight of our joint Designated Safeguarding Leads, Adam Byrne and Craig Lloyd, both Level 5 Safeguarding/DSL trained and Enhanced DBS checked, in line with KCSIE 2026 and our full Safeguarding & Compliance framework.
