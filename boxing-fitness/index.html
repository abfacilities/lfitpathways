🥊 Boxing Fitness & Discipline

Non-contact boxing training that provides structure, discipline and a healthy physical outlet. Proven to improve focus, respect and self-esteem, especially for young people who struggle to engage with conventional activities.

Make a Referral Safeguarding & Compliance

What Boxing Fitness & Discipline Involves

Boxing Fitness & Discipline sessions are strictly non-contact and centre on pad work, technique, fitness conditioning and structured discipline. The format gives young people a physical outlet with clear rules, clear structure and a visible sense of progress — qualities that translate directly into behaviour, focus and self-regulation outside the session too.

Sessions are supervised throughout by a qualified coach, with equipment condition checked before every session and technique built up gradually and safely.

Who This Is For

Behavioural Challenges

A structured physical outlet that channels energy and builds self-regulation.

Low Self-Esteem & Focus

Visible, achievable progress that builds genuine confidence and respect.

Disengagement From Conventional Activities

An alternative physical activity for young people who have not connected with traditional PE or sport.

NEET & Post-16 Re-engagement

A structured, disciplined routine that supports wider re-engagement goals.

Safeguarding & Delivery

All sessions are strictly non-contact, led by a qualified coach, with equipment condition checks before every session and a first aider present throughout.

As with every L-Fit Pathways provision, Boxing Fitness & Discipline is delivered under the direct oversight of our joint Designated Safeguarding Leads, Adam Byrne and Craig Lloyd, both Level 5 Safeguarding/DSL trained and Enhanced DBS checked, in line with KCSIE 2026 and our full Safeguarding & Compliance framework.
