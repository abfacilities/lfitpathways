🧩 Life Skills

Practical, real-world sessions covering communication, budgeting, cooking, personal safety, travel training and independence skills. Essential preparation for young people transitioning to adulthood, particularly those at risk of becoming NEET.

Make a Referral Safeguarding & Compliance

What Life Skills Involves

Life Skills sessions are hands-on and practical, covering the everyday skills that many young people leaving education without the right support can miss out on: budgeting, cooking, personal safety, communication and independent travel. Sessions are delivered both on-site and, where appropriate, in real community settings so that skills are practised in the environments young people will actually use them in.

Each young person's Life Skills plan is built around their own transition goals, whether that is preparing for college, an apprenticeship, employment, or simply greater independence at home.

Who This Is For

NEET Risk (Ages 16–25)

Practical, employment-readiness and independence skills for young people not in education, employment or training.

Transitioning to Adulthood

Preparation for the practical demands of independent living, college or the workplace.

Post-16 Disengagement

A structured, low-pressure re-engagement route built around real-world skills.

Additional Support Needs

Life skills broken into manageable, individually-paced steps for young people with SEND.

Safeguarding & Delivery

Off-site and community-based Life Skills sessions run to increased staff ratios and a pre-assessed route or destination, with staff checking in with a Designated Safeguarding Lead before and after any lone community-based session.

As with every L-Fit Pathways provision, Life Skills is delivered under the direct oversight of our joint Designated Safeguarding Leads, Adam Byrne and Craig Lloyd, both Level 5 Safeguarding/DSL trained and Enhanced DBS checked, in line with KCSIE 2026 and our full Safeguarding & Compliance framework.
